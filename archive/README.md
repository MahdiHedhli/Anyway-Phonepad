# Original PhonePad preservation files

Preserved 2026-10-11 at the owner's request. These are the original bytes of two uploaded PDF manuals and the uploaded Android companion APK, not recreated documents or download links.

- [PhonePad leaflet](manuals/PhonePad.pdf): two pages; FCC reference recorded in the manifest.
- [Anyware Products User Manual](manuals/AnyWare%20PhonePad%20PhoneBook%20Manual%20Manuel%20Tablette.pdf): 31 pages.
- [Anyware APKPure APK](software/Anyware_1.1.6145_APKPure.apk): version 1.1.6145 according to its original filename; package/signature verification remains pending.
- [Manifest](manifest.json): original filenames, exact sizes, SHA-256 hashes and provenance.
- [Checksums](SHA256SUMS): verify from this directory with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS` or `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.

## Integrity and trust

Each original upload was SHA-256 hashed in the conversation runtime. Existing copies on the owner's computer matched those hashes before being copied here. Files are stored directly in Git, not as Git LFS pointers. The APK ZIP CRC check passed, but that does not establish safety or vendor authenticity. It was not installed or executed. No firmware image has been recovered or backed up.

Original vendor materials retain their third-party copyrights and notices. Their presence is not an assertion of an open-source or redistribution license. Treat the APK as untrusted research material and preserve the stock device before any future flashing.
